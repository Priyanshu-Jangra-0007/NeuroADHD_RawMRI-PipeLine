## 6. Unresolved Scientific Questions & Governance Requirements

### Scientific Questions (Pending Supervisor Approval):
1. **Slice-Timing Correction**: Should multi-band or interleaved slice-timing correction be activated for high-resolution pilot data, or kept bypassed to preserve exact C-PAC benchmark concordance?
2. **Susceptibility Distortion Correction (SDC)**: Should phase-reversed spin-echo fieldmap unwarping be required for prospective pilot scans?
3. **Facial Defacing Boundary**: Supervisor sign-off required confirming defacing tools do not truncate orbitofrontal gray matter.
4. **QC Numerical Thresholds**: 8 proposed QC acceptance thresholds (e.g., Coregistration NMI $\ge 0.55$, SyN Dice $\ge 0.85$, Mean FD $\le 0.50\text{ mm}$, tSNR $\ge 40$) are designated `PENDING_SUPERVISOR_APPROVAL`.

### Governance Requirements:
1. **IRB Protocol Authorization**: Formal institutional review board protocol approval or exemption determination.
2. **Data Use Agreement (DUA)**: Executed research DUA for the target pilot cohort.
3. **Master Key-Linking Table Isolation**: Physical air-gapping of patient identity mapping table.

## 7. Blocking Issues

The following 4 items represent blocking requirements before real MRI data can be processed:

1. **Stand-Alone Model Artifact Packaging**:
   - The frozen model weights (`final_model.joblib`) and pipeline (`final_preprocessing_pipeline.joblib`) reside in the sibling benchmark directory and are excluded from version control by `.gitignore: *.joblib`.
   - *Requirement*: A secure model volume mount (`/opt/neuro_adhd/models:ro`) or artifact retrieval protocol must be deployed for containerized inference.
2. **Supervisor QC Threshold Sign-Off**:
   - The 8 QC acceptance thresholds marked `PENDING_SUPERVISOR_APPROVAL` must be formally approved.
3. **IRB / Institutional DUA Approval**:
   - Research ethics and legal clearance must be granted.
4. **Code-Level Real-Data Enablement**:
   - `check_safety_guards()` strictly blocks non-synthetic datasets and raises errors on real scan paths. A dedicated, separately reviewed enablement phase is required to permit real scans.
